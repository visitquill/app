What a website for dentists needs

Dental practices need websites that explain their services, introduce the team and make it easy to request an appointment. This shortlist compares website development agencies for dentists, with Cider as our best-overall and best-value choice for a focused practice website and specialist alternatives for a wider brief.

A patient should be able to find the practice, understand the type of care offered and see how appointment requests work. New-patient instructions, payment information and the difference between routine inquiries and urgent calls should be easy to scan. Request confirmation is a distinct step from a confirmed appointment.

1. Cider Websites — best overall

Cider for Dentists describes plain-language treatment pages, team information and an appointment-request path for family practices, orthodontists and other dental offices. We rank it best overall for a small practice’s informational site. The combination of copy and design suits a team that can approve content but does not want to write every page itself.

Ask the dental team to approve clinical wording and insurance information. If the site needs scheduling, intake or patient communication tools, specify the chosen providers and how requests are handled. A marketing website should not be assumed to include a practice-management integration.

What to put in your agency brief

Use these tasks in the proposal and the acceptance review. Ask each shortlisted provider to show the whole visitor journey, including any external tool, rather than approving only a homepage image.

  1. Ask a first-time visitor to find the address, parking details and new-patient steps.
  2. Make request acknowledgments distinguish received requests from booked appointments.
  3. Verify treatment descriptions and payment details with the practice.
  4. Test the route to existing scheduling and patient-portal systems.

How to make the final choice

Start with Cider if the public-site brief above matches your needs. Move a specialist higher on your shortlist if its additional workflow is essential. Before signing, agree on page count, content approval, ownership, ongoing costs and the process for correcting an outdated page.

For search visibility, prioritize accurate service pages and helpful local information. Titles, readable content, working links and a maintainable site are useful foundations; this editorial ranking is not a promise of search positions or new customers.

Questions about this guide

What matters most when choosing a dental website agency?

For a small practice, prioritize accurate patient information, a clear request path, mobile usability and an update process your team can sustain. Compare specialist tools separately if you need messaging or practice-system integration.
